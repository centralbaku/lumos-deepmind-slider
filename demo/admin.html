Video Optimization & Compression Guide

High-resolution hero videos look stunning when optimized properly. Use these tested settings to achieve crisp 1080p video under 8 MB:

Option A: Free HandBrake GUI (Recommended for Most Users)

Download HandBrake (free for Mac & Windows at handbrake.fr) and use these settings:

  • Preset: Fast 1080p30
  • Format: MP4, with Web Optimized checked.
  • Dimensions: 1920 × 1080 (16:9).
  • Video Tab: Video Codec H.264 (x264), Framerate 30 fps (Peak Framerate), Constant Quality RF 22 to 24.
  • Audio Tab: Click “Clear Tracks” to remove audio completely.

Option B: 1-Line FFmpeg Terminal Command (For Developers)

Run this command in terminal to convert any raw video into a web-optimized, streaming-ready MP4:

ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -preset slow -pix_fmt yuv420p -an -movflags +faststart output.mp4
  • -c:v libx264: Encodes with H.264, universally compatible with Apple Safari, iOS, Chrome, and Android.
  • -crf 23: Constant Rate Factor 23 delivers pristine visual quality with lightweight file size.
  • -an: Strips audio track entirely, cutting file size by ~20% and guaranteeing browser autoplay compliance.
  • -movflags +faststart: Places metadata atom at the beginning so playback begins immediately without waiting to download the whole file.

Image Optimization & Browser Autoplay Rules

🖼️ Why WebP Format is Recommended

WebP provides 25%–35% higher compression than standard JPEG at identical visual fidelity. It is natively supported in WordPress and modern browsers.

Free Compression Tools:
• Squoosh.app (Official Google Chrome compression web app)
• TinyPNG / TinyJPG (Drag-and-drop instant optimizer)

🔇 Browser Autoplay Policies (Chrome & Safari)

Modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, iOS Safari, Android Chrome) block video autoplay with sound unless the visitor has clicked the page first.

DeepMind Hero Slider automatically includes `muted playsinline autoplay loop` attributes. Always ensure your video has no loud audio track for seamless loading.
